##  Pricing Plans

### ✅ Plan 1: Basic – ₹55,000
Best for essential features with minimal complexity.

**Features:**
- **Public Website:**
  - Homepage with slider, About us, Terms & Conditions, Team photos
  - Admission page (shift & duration details)
  - Readmission page (shift & duration details)
  - Login page (OTP-based authentication)
- **Student Management:**
  - Aadhaar upload (online & office)
  - Admission & renewal via mobile number / Aadhaar
- **Payments & Receipts:**
  - UPI integration
  - Cash workflow with admin approval
  - PDF receipt generation & download
- **Admin Dashboard:**
  - View/manage students
  - Approve cash payments
  - Basic dashboards (enrolled/active students, revenue, dues)
- **Notifications:**
  - SMS reminders 2 days before & after due date (with payment link)
- **Renewals & Readmissions:**
  - Auto-renewal via mobile/Aadhaar
- **Locker Management:**
  - Online locker booking system
- **Discount System:**
  - Early bird and referral discount management

**Excludes:** News API, batch management, advanced analytics.

⏳ **Timeline:** 10 weeks

---

**Includes all Basic features plus:**

  - Daily admission reports
  - Monthly admission reports
  - Fee collection reports
  - Shift-wise statistics
- **Website Content Writing** (About Us, T&C, Team Bios) – *₹7,500 included*

### 🟣 Plan 3: Premium – ₹95,000
Complete feature set with support.

**Includes all Standard features plus:**

  - Batch-wise reports

  - Detailed student reports with filters
  - Payment history (cash & UPI)
  - Non-renewal reports
  - Advanced analytics and filtering
- **Referral Dashboard**
- **Priority Support** for 6 months (bug fixes, updates)

⏳ **Timeline:** 14 weeks
