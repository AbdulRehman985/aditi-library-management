## ✅ Advantages of WordPress Plan
- 🚀 **Faster delivery** – ready in 1-2 weeks
- 💸 **Lower initial cost** – fits within your budget
- 🖥️ Easy to manage using WordPress admin panel
- 🔌 Access to thousands of plugins for extra features
